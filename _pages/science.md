My research focuses on astronomical instrumentation and polarimetry. I develop novel optical instruments for precision measurements of astrophysical magnetic fields and the interstellar medium.

## Current Research

### WALOP
Wide-field All-sky Linear Optical Polarimeter (WALOP) is designed to perform large-area optical polarimetric surveys.

### FiberPol
FiberPol is a next-generation fibre-fed polarimeter aimed at achieving high-precision spectropolarimetry.

## Research Interests

- Astronomical instrumentation
- Polarimetry
- Spectroscopy
- Interstellar dust
- Optical design
