## Astronomical Instrumentation
**University of Cape Town (2024)**

I co-developed and taught the inaugural Master's-level course in **Astronomical Instrumentation** at the University of Cape Town. The course introduced the principles behind modern astronomical instruments, covering topics from telescope optics and detectors to spectroscopy, polarimetry, calibration, and instrument design.
